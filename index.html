Frequently asked questions

Questions homeowners usually have.

A few straightforward answers before you request service.

How do I know which service I need?
If you're unsure, describe the problem in the request form. The service team can use those details to clarify the appropriate service category and next step.
Can I request service for more than one system?
Yes. Mention the additional systems in the notes field so the request can be understood as a whole rather than as separate unrelated problems.
Do I need to know exactly what is wrong?
No. A description of what you are experiencing is often enough to start the conversation. Avoid attempting repairs yourself if you believe the situation may be unsafe.
How is scheduling handled?
After receiving the request, the appropriate team member can confirm availability and coordinate a suitable time.
What information should I include in my request?
Include the service you think you need, what you have noticed, your ZIP code, and any useful timing information. Photos or additional details can be requested later if appropriate.
Is the estimate automatically a service appointment?
No. A submitted request is an initial inquiry. Service availability, scope and appointment timing should be confirmed before work begins.
